# 100 fresh .com names

This replaces the previous rejected shortlist. I checked **370 distinct candidates**: 171 had registration records, 197 returned no registration record, and 2 remained inconclusive. The 100 below were selected from the names with no record, for variety, pronunciation, and fit with a private social network.

**Registry checks: 17 Sept 2026, 12:09 to 17 Sept 2026, 12:15, Singapore time.** Each listed domain returned HTTP 404 from Verisign’s official .com RDAP endpoint. That makes these possible registration candidates, not confirmed registrar offers. Prices and trademark clearance have not been checked.

Names use capital letters below to make the words easier to read; domain spelling is case-insensitive. The list favors ordinary words and short phrases, without digits or hyphens. All 100 are fresh relative to the previous shortlist.

## Method and limits

The .com registry endpoint was resolved through [IANA’s official RDAP bootstrap file](https://data.iana.org/rdap/dns.json). Registration checks used the exact domain names, not DNS resolution or website errors. A registered result required HTTP 200 and a matching domain in the registry response. A no-record result required HTTP 404 and either the registry’s error object or an empty response body. Timeouts and other failures were kept as inconclusive and excluded from the 100. Lookup links can therefore show a blank or not-found response.

These are naming candidates, not a full brand or trademark clearance. A few promising names received exploratory web searches; that is not a comprehensive search of every name. I left out TheFriendsWeKeep.com because the phrase is already the title of [Jane Green’s novel](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/540213/the-friends-we-keep-by-jane-green/), and OurSavePoint.com because an existing [gaming and family-life blog](https://note.com/unagiinu_0627/n/n6d94d45aaa9d?hl=en) uses that exact title. Other names can still have existing uses.

A registrar must confirm availability and purchase/renewal price before registration. The app remains named Common; no domains have been bought.

## Latest naming direction — 17 September 2026

Danny singled out SameOldFaces.com and KnownInPerson.com, then proposed little or safe + a synonym for face + a synonym for book, and words Gen Z or Gen Alpha use for friends. No name has been chosen and no domain has been purchased.

The discussion explored fam, besties, crew, and homies. Literal face synonyms tend to be awkward, so looser combinations with smiles, friends, pages, albums, and stories were also considered. Little was preferred by the assistant over safe for a warmer tone; this is a naming opinion, not a user decision.
